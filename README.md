Entry for the make.fun bounty [Monday, Reviewed in One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-monday-reviewed-in-one-line-e55de3c1): the funniest one-line review of my own Monday, under 200 characters, and nothing else.

> Monday walked in like it owned the place, spilled my coffee, and scheduled a meeting about the spill.

`main.ts` writes exactly that line (101 characters, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: no randomness, no network, no clock.
